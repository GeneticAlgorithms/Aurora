* Aurora
  -----
* Aurora is a hack that I pulled together on 10/8/26 at Imbue in SF.
* Its a take on enhancing the math education experience for neurodivergent learners (such as thosew with ADHD) who may benefit more from interactive and sensory methods to learn.
* It combines math concepts and the allure of electronic dance music and cathartic raves to increase attention and encoding.
